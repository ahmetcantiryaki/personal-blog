Short answer: Claude Academy is Anthropic's free, public learning hub, launched on August 20, 2026, that replaced the developer-only Anthropic Academy. It offers over 350 resources — courses, tutorials, use cases, and webinars — covering everything from AI basics to API-level development, and you can browse it without creating an account.

## What is Claude Academy?

Claude Academy is Anthropic's rebuilt education platform, released on August 20, 2026, as a full rebrand and expansion of the earlier Anthropic Academy. Where the old version targeted developers only, Claude Academy is built for anyone — from someone who has never used an AI assistant to an engineer building on the [Claude Platform](/en/posts/claude-files-api-ga-explained).

The hub is free, requires no credit card, and does not require sign-in just to browse the catalog. According to early coverage, it hosts roughly 355 total resources as of its launch, spanning self-paced courses, shorter tutorials, real-world use cases, and recorded webinars. Anthropic structured the content in three tiers: AI fundamentals for newcomers, product-specific learning paths, and technical, API-level material for developers.

## What does Claude Academy cover?

Claude Academy covers five distinct product tracks plus a foundational layer on AI basics, according to [Superhuman AI's report on the launch](https://www.superhuman.ai/p/anthropic-launches-the-claude-academy-with-355-resources). Each track is meant to take a learner from "what is this tool" to practical, hands-on use.

| Track | Focus |
|---|---|
| AI fundamentals | General AI literacy for non-technical learners |
| Claude.ai | Using Claude's consumer chat product day to day |
| Claude Code | Agentic coding workflows in the terminal and IDE |
| Claude Cowork | Collaborative, workspace-style agent use |
| Claude Tag | Product-specific workflows under the Tag branding |
| Claude (Developer) Platform / API | Building with the API, tool use, and integrations |

Reports on the exact resource breakdown vary. [BiGGo's coverage](https://finance.biggo.com/news/1456ce43-1d49-438e-a24e-a7ae72c2668e) cites around 22 self-paced courses, 119 tutorials, 148 use cases, and 66 webinars, while others simply describe "over 350 resources, including roughly 20-plus courses." Treat the headline total — over 350 — as the reliable figure and the internal split as approximate. Some lessons include hands-on practical exercises, and structured courses still award completion certificates, a feature carried over directly from the original Anthropic Academy.

The platform is also available in multiple languages, including Japanese, which signals Anthropic's push to make Claude education accessible outside English-speaking markets.

## How is Claude Academy different from the old Anthropic Academy?

The core difference is audience and access: Anthropic Academy was a developer-only catalog hosted on a third-party platform, while Claude Academy is a free, general-audience hub built to sit closer to Anthropic's own products. Anthropic Academy launched in March 2026 on Skilljar with a much smaller catalog focused narrowly on Claude Code, the API, and MCP.

| | Anthropic Academy (Mar 2026) | Claude Academy (Aug 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Audience | Developers only | Everyone, beginner to developer |
| Hosting | Third-party (Skilljar) | Anthropic-owned hub |
| Sign-in to browse | Required | Not required |
| Catalog size | ~13–18 courses | ~355 resources total |
| Product coverage | Claude Code, API, MCP | Claude.ai, Code, Cowork, Tag, Platform/API |
| Languages | English-focused | Multiple, including Japanese |

That expansion in scope is the real story. Anthropic Academy proved there was developer demand for structured Claude training; Claude Academy is a bet that the same appeal works for a much wider, non-technical audience too. If you already work with Claude Code day to day, pairing the academy's material with practical guides — like a breakdown of [Claude Code subagents and background agents](/en/posts/claude-code-subagents-background-agents) — will likely get you further than the courses alone.

## Why did Anthropic launch it now?

Claude Academy landed in the same general week as two other Anthropic announcements: the permanent $2/$10 per-million-token intro pricing for Claude Sonnet 5 (confirmed August 11, 2026, reversing a planned rise to $3/$15 on September 1), and the general availability of computer use, browser use, Files, and Skills APIs on the Claude Platform. None of these are officially linked, but the sequencing reads as a coordinated push to lower the barrier to adopting Claude — cheaper models, more GA capabilities, and now free education to explain how to use them.

Free developer education is not a new tactic — cloud providers and API companies have run certification programs for years — but launching a no-sign-in, non-developer academy the same month as major API and pricing news suggests Anthropic is treating education as a growth lever, not a side project. It is a reasonable bet: the more people who understand what Claude Cowork or the [Claude Files API](/en/posts/claude-files-api-ga-explained) actually does, the larger the pool of people who might pay to use it. That does not make it a bad academy — free, well-structured learning material is a genuine benefit regardless of the motive — but it is worth naming that Claude Academy also functions as a funnel into Anthropic's paid products.

## How do you sign up for Claude Academy?

You do not need to sign up to browse the Claude Academy catalog — the resources are viewable for free without an account, according to [reports on the launch](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-launches-claude-academy/). Anthropic has not published a separate signup flow distinct from its existing account system, and completion certificates for structured courses likely require signing in with a standard Anthropic account, consistent with how the earlier Anthropic Academy handled certification.

Given that pricing and product details can shift after launch, check [Anthropic's own newsroom](https://www.anthropic.com/news) for the current sign-in requirements and course list before assuming any detail here is still accurate months later.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Claude Academy free?

Yes. Claude Academy is free to browse and use, with no credit card required, according to every outlet that covered the August 20, 2026 launch. Some structured courses that award completion certificates may still require a free Anthropic account sign-in, similar to how the earlier Anthropic Academy worked.

### How many courses does Claude Academy have?

Reports describe roughly 355 total resources as of launch, with one breakdown citing about 22 self-paced courses alongside 119 tutorials, 148 use cases, and 66 webinars. Different outlets split the numbers slightly differently, so treat "over 350 resources, including 20-plus courses" as the safer summary rather than any single exact breakdown.

### What is the difference between Claude Academy and Anthropic Academy?

Anthropic Academy, launched in March 2026 on Skilljar, was a developer-only catalog of roughly 13 to 18 courses on Claude Code, the API, and MCP. Claude Academy, launched August 20, 2026, rebuilt and expanded that catalog into a free, general-audience hub with around 355 resources covering five product tracks, from AI fundamentals to API-level work.

### Does Claude Academy cover Claude Code and the API?

Yes. Claude Academy includes dedicated learning paths for Claude Code and the Claude (Developer) Platform/API, alongside tracks for Claude.ai, Claude Cowork, and Claude Tag. Developers who want deeper technical grounding can pair the academy's API track with resources like a [Python SDK v1 migration guide](/en/posts/anthropic-python-sdk-v1-migration-guide) or a guide to [choosing the right Claude model in 2026](/en/posts/which-claude-model-2026).
