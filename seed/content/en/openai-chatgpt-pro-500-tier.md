Short answer: OpenAI announced a new $500/month "ChatGPT Pro 500" tier at DevDay on September 29, 2026, giving subscribers 25x the usage of Plus and exclusive access to an "Ultrafast" inference mode. At the same event, OpenAI confirmed that the existing $200/month Pro plan's usage allowance for Work and Codex tasks is being cut in half starting October 30, 2026.

## What exactly did OpenAI announce?

A new top tier was added above the existing Pro plan, and the existing Pro plan's usage got smaller at the same time. The new ladder runs: Plus at $20/month (1x baseline usage), Pro 100 at $100/month (5x), Pro 200 at $200/month (10x usage, down from roughly 20x before the change), and the new Pro 500 at $500/month (25x usage, plus exclusive access to Ultrafast mode).

That Pro 200 number is the part worth sitting with: it's the same $200 price point that existed before DevDay, now delivering roughly half the usage allowance it used to. OpenAI isn't calling this a price increase, because the sticker price didn't move — but the usage-per-dollar at that tier dropped by half.

## What does $500/month actually buy you?

Access to Ultrafast mode, which is the tier's real differentiator rather than the usage multiplier alone. Ultrafast runs GPT-6 Astra at up to 300 tokens per second inside Codex — roughly 8x faster than the standard inference speed — and as of this announcement, Pro 500 is the only subscription tier that includes it.

| Tier | Price/month | Usage multiplier | Ultrafast access |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plus | $20 | 1x | No |
| Pro 100 | $100 | 5x | No |
| Pro 200 | $200 (was ~20x, now 10x) | 10x | No |
| Pro 500 | $500 | 25x | Yes |

## Why does this pricing ladder look the way it does?

Because OpenAI has been widening the top of its subscription range for years, and this is the next rung rather than a one-off. ChatGPT Plus has anchored the bottom at $20/month since 2023. ChatGPT Pro launched in December 2024 at $200/month, built specifically to gate access to o1 pro mode — the first time OpenAI priced a ChatGPT tier an order of magnitude above Plus. The September 2026 restructuring repeats that same move one level up: Pro 500 gates access to Ultrafast the same way the original $200 Pro plan gated access to o1 pro mode two years earlier.

Reading the ladder this way makes the Pro 200 usage cut look less like an isolated decision and more like a predictable side effect of adding a new top tier: once a $500 option exists, the $200 tier's old "effectively unlimited for most users" usage allowance stops making sense as the second-highest tier, so OpenAI resized it downward to create daylight between Pro 200 and Pro 500 for anyone willing to pay more.

## Why is OpenAI cutting the $200 Pro plan's usage?

OpenAI hasn't given a public rationale beyond the pricing-tier restructuring itself, but the practical effect is a usage downgrade at an unchanged price for every existing Pro subscriber, timed to push the heaviest users toward the new $500 tier instead. Existing $200 subscribers keep their current, higher limits until October 29, 2026 — the cut takes effect October 30 — and OpenAI is giving every existing Pro subscriber a one-time grant of $2,500 in usage credits, expiring at the end of 2026, as a cushion for the transition.

That credit grant is a meaningful detail: $2,500 in usage credits for a plan that costs $200/month is roughly a year's worth of subscription cost handed back in compute, which suggests OpenAI expects real pushback from its heaviest Codex and Work users once the halved allowance actually bites.

## Who should actually consider the $500 tier?

Developers running latency-sensitive Codex workloads where the 8x Ultrafast speedup changes what's practical — an agentic coding loop that depends on fast iteration is the clearest case — and teams that were already exceeding the old Pro 200 usage ceiling before the cut. For anyone whose usage sat comfortably under the new 10x limit, the $200 plan doesn't change in practice; the cut only bites once you were relying on the roughly 20x allowance that existed before September 29.

For everyone else, the one-time $2,500 credit buys time to actually measure usage against the new ceiling before deciding whether to upgrade, downgrade, or switch providers entirely — a decision worth revisiting against a broader [AI subscription comparison](/en/posts/which-ai-subscription-2026) rather than in isolation.

## Is this a fair trade, or a stealth price increase?

My honest read: calling this "not a price increase" is technically true and practically misleading. The dollar amount on the $200 tier didn't move, but the usage-per-dollar did, which is the number that actually determines what a heavy user can do with their subscription. Pairing that cut with a brand-new $500 tier that includes the one genuinely new capability — Ultrafast — reads less like an unrelated restructuring and more like a deliberate funnel toward the higher tier.

The $2,500 credit grant is the detail that keeps this from looking purely extractive — it's a real, substantial cushion, not a token gesture — but it's also temporary, expiring at the end of 2026, which means the actual test of this pricing change comes in January 2027, once the credits run out and Pro 200 users are living with the halved allowance permanently. Anyone budgeting AI tooling costs for next year should treat that date, not October 30, as the one to plan around.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When does the ChatGPT Pro 200 usage cut take effect?

The usage cut to the existing $200/month Pro plan's Work and Codex allowance takes effect October 30, 2026. Existing subscribers keep their current, higher limits until October 29.

### What is Ultrafast mode in ChatGPT?

Ultrafast is an inference mode that runs GPT-6 Astra at up to 300 tokens per second inside Codex, roughly 8x the standard inference speed. As of the September 29, 2026 announcement, it's exclusive to the new $500/month Pro 500 tier.

### Do existing ChatGPT Pro subscribers get any compensation for the usage cut?

Yes. OpenAI is giving every existing $200/month Pro subscriber a one-time grant of $2,500 in usage credits, which expire at the end of 2026, to offset the reduced usage allowance during the transition.

### How much more usage does ChatGPT Pro 500 include compared to Plus?

Pro 500 includes roughly 25x the usage allowance of the $20/month Plus plan, compared to Pro 200's 10x (down from about 20x before the September 29, 2026 change) and Pro 100's 5x.
