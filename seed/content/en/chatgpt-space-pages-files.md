Short answer: ChatGPT Space is OpenAI's replacement for the Library tab — one home for your pages, uploaded files and project work — rolling out since OpenAI's September 29, 2026 DevDay to Pro, Business and Enterprise accounts on web and desktop. Free, Go and Plus accounts still use Library with no announced change date, and mobile can only find, read and share Space pages for now, not create or edit them.

## What exactly is ChatGPT Space, and what did it replace?

Space took over the role the Library tab used to play: it's where your pages, your uploaded files, and anything else you've built inside ChatGPT now live, in one browsable surface instead of a flat history list. Projects stay separate and keep working exactly as before — project-scoped chats, files and custom instructions haven't moved anywhere. The practical distinction is that a Project is a scoped workspace for one ongoing thread of work, while Space is where the artifacts you actually produced — a page, a brief, a deck — live so you can find and share them later. It's a narrower, more structured version of the same instinct behind [organizing AI chats and Gems](/en/posts/organize-ai-chats-and-gems): stop letting output pile up in an undifferentiated history.

## Who actually has ChatGPT Space right now?

Access depends entirely on plan, and as of late November 2026 the split is clean rather than gradual.

| Plan | Has Space? | Platform notes |
|---|---|---|
| Free / Go | No — keeps Library | OpenAI hasn't announced a change date |
| Plus | No — keeps Library | OpenAI hasn't announced a change date |
| Pro | Yes | Web and desktop; mobile is read/share only |
| Business | Yes | Web and desktop; mobile is read/share only |
| Enterprise | Yes | Web and desktop; data-residency regions (Canada, UAE) coming soon |

If your plan isn't in the "Yes" rows, there's nothing to configure — you're not missing a setting, the feature simply hasn't reached your tier yet.

## Do pages in Space actually refresh themselves from connected apps?

Not yet, and this is worth knowing before you build a workflow that assumes otherwise. "Keep updated" — the feature that would auto-refresh a page's content from a connected app — isn't available at launch. As of late November 2026, a page is a snapshot from when it was created or last edited, not a live view into a connected document. Plan around that: if a page needs to reflect the latest version of something, you still have to update it yourself for now.

## How do file permissions work inside a page?

A file you upload directly into a page follows that page's own sharing permissions — share the page, and whoever you shared it with can see the file. A file pulled in from a connected service, such as a shared drive, keeps the permissions of the original service instead. That split matters in practice: sharing a Space page never accidentally widens access to a connected file someone else locked down elsewhere, which is the failure mode you'd actually worry about with a shared workspace.

## Can I bring Slides into Space yet?

Partially. Slides is rolling out to eligible accounts that already have Space access, so whether you see it depends on your specific account rather than just your plan tier. Sheets is described as "coming soon" rather than live, so don't design a workflow around a Space-native spreadsheet yet — for now, a connected spreadsheet behaves like any other connected file: visible in the page, permissioned by its source.

## What does organizing a real project in Space look like end to end?

Take a product-launch project as a worked example. Start by creating one page as the project's hub rather than scattering files across separate chats — this is the single biggest habit change from the old Library model. Upload the core documents directly into that page: the launch brief, early copy drafts, the asset checklist. Pull in anything that lives in a connected service, such as a shared product spec, rather than re-uploading a copy — that keeps one source of truth and preserves the original file's permissions automatically. If your account has Slides, draft the internal pitch deck directly inside the page instead of exporting back and forth from a separate tool. When the page is ready, share it with the team, keeping in mind that teammates checking it from their phones can read and comment but can't edit until they're back on web or desktop. This single-hub approach is the same instinct behind [building a 30-day content calendar with ChatGPT](/en/posts/30-day-content-calendar-chatgpt) — one place to find everything related to a project beats re-finding it across a dozen old chats.

## Who should actually move to Space today?

If you're on Free, Go or Plus, there's nothing to decide yet — stay on Library until OpenAI announces a date, since there's no manual opt-in. If you're on Pro, Business or Enterprise, Space is already your default, and the real decision is making sure your team treats Projects and Space as two different tools rather than conflating them: a Project for an ongoing thread of work, Space for the finished or in-progress artifacts that thread produces. Getting that split right from week one avoids the cleanup problem covered in [fixing ChatGPT's "memory full" and using Projects right](/en/posts/chatgpt-memory-full-projects-guide), where everything piles into one place because nobody drew the line early.

My honest take: the most useful part of this change isn't Slides or the file browser, it's that OpenAI finally gave "the thing I made in ChatGPT" a permanent address instead of a position in a scrolling history — that alone makes Space worth the switch for Pro, Business and Enterprise accounts, pending features aside.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does ChatGPT Space replace Projects?

No. Space replaces the Library tab and is where your pages, uploaded files and other work live; Projects remain a separate feature for scoped chats, files and custom instructions tied to one ongoing piece of work.

### Which ChatGPT plans have access to Space?

Pro, Business and Enterprise accounts have Space on web and desktop as of late November 2026. Free, Go and Plus accounts still use Library, and OpenAI hasn't announced a date for that to change.

### Can I edit ChatGPT Space pages from my phone?

Not yet. Mobile currently supports finding, reading and sharing Space pages, but creating or editing them requires web or desktop.

### Do ChatGPT Space pages update automatically when a connected file changes?

No, not at launch. The "Keep updated" feature that would auto-refresh a page from a connected app isn't available yet, so a page reflects its content as of when it was created or last edited, not the live state of a connected source.
