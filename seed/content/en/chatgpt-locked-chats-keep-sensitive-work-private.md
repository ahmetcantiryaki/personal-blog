Short answer: ChatGPT's Locked Chats, rolled out October 20, 2026, let you hide specific conversations from your regular history, pinned chats, and search behind a 4-digit PIN or Face ID/Touch ID. They're a privacy screen for one device, not encryption — anyone who unlocks the chat can still read and copy everything inside it.

## What exactly do Locked Chats hide — and what do they not hide?

A locked chat disappears from your main chat list, your pinned chats, and ChatGPT's search results; it only reappears inside a separate "Locked chats" area under Projects, after you unlock it. What it does not do is add end-to-end encryption or change your data retention settings — the conversation is stored on OpenAI's servers exactly as any other chat would be.

That distinction matters because it's easy to read "locked" as "encrypted." Per [OpenAI's help article on Locked Chats](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001411-using-locked-chats-in-chatgpt), locking only controls visibility inside the ChatGPT interface — not storage, not training-data settings, and not what a screenshot or a copy-paste can expose once the chat is open. Locked Chats also aren't available on ChatGPT for Healthcare as of this writing.

## How do you lock a chat and set a PIN?

Open the chat you want to hide, tap its three-dot menu (•••), and choose "Lock chat." The first time you lock any conversation, ChatGPT asks you to create a 4-digit PIN — that single PIN protects every locked chat on the account, not just the one you're locking.

The rollout is gradual. If "Lock chat" isn't in that menu yet, OpenAI's help center says the feature simply hasn't reached your account — there's no separate opt-in waitlist to join. Coverage from [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/20/chatgpt-now-lets-you-lock-your-chats/) and OpenAI's own documentation both describe the rollout as reaching Free, Go, Plus, and Pro accounts first, with OpenAI's help center also listing Business, Enterprise, and Edu plans.

## Can you open Locked Chats with Face ID or Touch ID instead of a PIN?

Yes, on supported mobile devices. Turning on device authentication lets you unlock with Face ID, Touch ID, or your phone's screen lock instead of typing the PIN every time. That convenience setting is per-device — enabling it on your phone doesn't carry over to your laptop — and changing the account PIN still requires the current PIN even when biometrics normally cover day-to-day unlocking. On web and desktop, typing the PIN is the only option right now.

## When should you actually use Locked Chats at work?

Lock a chat whenever its content would be a problem if a coworker glanced at your screen, borrowed your laptop, or scrolled your chat history by accident. That covers client details under an NDA, HR complaint drafts, legal strategy notes, salary negotiation prep, and performance review drafts — anything whose exposure creates a real consequence, not just mild embarrassment.

| Scenario | Lock it? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Drafting a salary negotiation script | Yes | Numbers you don't want a colleague to see on a shared screen |
| Summarizing a client NDA | Yes | Confidentiality risk if a screenshot leaks |
| HR complaint or legal strategy notes | Yes | Real professional consequence if exposed |
| Brainstorming blog post ideas | No | No sensitive data, locking adds friction for nothing |
| Regulated healthcare or financial client data | No — use an enterprise workspace instead | Locked Chats aren't a compliance control |

## What happens if you forget your PIN?

This is the real risk in the feature: resetting a forgotten PIN permanently deletes every locked chat on the account, along with anything stored only inside them, and OpenAI support cannot recover that content afterward, per the same help article. Treat the PIN the way you'd treat an unrecoverable encryption key — write it down somewhere safer than your memory, not somewhere a coworker could find it.

## Are Locked Chats a substitute for enterprise DLP or a private workspace?

No. Locked Chats control what you personally see on your own screen; they don't give IT administrators audit logs, e-discovery holds, or data-loss-prevention enforcement, and a workspace admin cannot reset your PIN or read your locked content. For data that's actually regulated — client financial records, protected health information, anything under a formal retention policy — the right tool is a managed [Business or Enterprise workspace](/en/posts/chatgpt-business-premium-seats-explained) with its own data controls, not a personal 4-digit PIN.

## How do Locked Chats pair with separate work and personal ChatGPT accounts?

Locked Chats work best as a second layer on top of keeping work and personal ChatGPT accounts separate, not as a replacement for that separation. If you already [connect work and personal ChatGPT accounts](/en/posts/connect-work-personal-accounts-chatgpt) and switch between them, lock the occasional sensitive chat inside each account rather than relying on locking alone to keep work content away from your personal history, or vice versa.

My honest take: Locked Chats are a well-designed screen lock, nothing more — exactly the right tool for "don't let my desk neighbor read this," and the wrong tool for "keep this compliant." The feature's biggest practical risk isn't a stranger breaking in; it's a forgotten PIN wiping content you needed. Before relying on it for anything irreplaceable, check your plan's details in the [ChatGPT plan comparison](/en/posts/chatgpt-complete-guide-2026) and decide whether a managed workspace fits the data better than a personal lock. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I lock a chat in ChatGPT?

Open the chat, tap its three-dot menu (•••), and select "Lock chat." The first time you do this you'll set a 4-digit PIN that protects every locked chat on your account, and from then on you'll find locked conversations under a separate "Locked chats" area inside Projects.

### Does locking a chat in ChatGPT encrypt it?

No. Locking a chat only hides it from your regular history, pinned chats, and search inside the ChatGPT interface — it does not add end-to-end encryption, and it does not change how OpenAI stores or retains the conversation on its servers.

### What happens if I forget my ChatGPT PIN?

Resetting a forgotten PIN permanently deletes every locked chat on your account along with anything stored only inside them, and OpenAI support cannot recover that content afterward. There's no backup PIN or recovery code, so treat the PIN like an unrecoverable key.

### Is ChatGPT Locked Chats available on the free plan?

Yes. OpenAI's rollout, which started October 20, 2026, includes Free, Go, Plus, and Pro accounts, and the feature's help documentation also lists Business, Enterprise, and Edu plans — though Locked Chats are excluded from ChatGPT for Healthcare and the rollout is gradual, so not every eligible account sees it on day one.
