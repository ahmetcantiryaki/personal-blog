Short answer: claim your business through OpenAI's Business Hub, which opened in beta on November 10, 2026, then complete Persona identity verification. Once approved, your profile gets a blue checkmark, you can see how often ChatGPT mentions you, and — if you qualify — you can launch a Business Agent. The process is free right now, though Business Agents are initially limited to eligible US ecommerce and online-service businesses.

Think of it as ChatGPT's answer to a Google Business Profile, except the goal isn't ranking in a map result — it's being named correctly when a user asks ChatGPT about you directly.

## What is Business Hub?

Business Hub is the dashboard OpenAI opened for companies to manage their presence inside ChatGPT. You can update your business details, track analytics showing how often customers see and click through to your profile, and, if eligible, set up a Business Agent. Existing OpenAI Enterprise customers and advertisers go through this process with their OpenAI representative instead of applying directly through Business Hub.

## How do you claim your business?

You claim your business in Business Hub by confirming you own or are authorized to represent it, and OpenAI assigns the profile to you once that link is approved. There are four ways to verify the claim: Persona identity verification, phone verification, domain verification (adding a TXT record that ChatGPT displays to your DNS), or connecting a Shopify store. Shopify merchants can link their store from Business Hub or straight from the Shopify admin to speed things up.

Claim approval and Persona identity verification are two separate steps. The blue checkmark next to your business name appears only once Persona approves you — not when OpenAI approves the claim itself. You need to finish Persona verification before you can publish profile changes, view analytics, or create a Business Agent.

```text
sample dns txt record (domain verification)
Name: _chatgpt-verification.yourdomain.com
Type: TXT
Value: the unique code Business Hub displays for your claim
```

## What do the analytics actually show?

Business Hub reports three core metrics: how often your business gets mentioned in ChatGPT's answers, how many times users view your profile, and how many of those visitors click through to your website. Together, these give you the first concrete feedback on how visible your brand is inside ChatGPT's answers — a space you previously had zero visibility into.

| Metric | What it measures | How to improve it |
|---|---|---|
| Mention frequency | How often your brand name appears in ChatGPT answers | Consistent name/product info, a complete profile |
| Profile views | How many times users opened your profile | A full business description, a clear category |
| Click-throughs | How many visitors went from your profile to your site | A clear CTA, correct URL, current contact info |

## What is a Business Agent, and who can build one?

A Business Agent is an AI agent users can chat with directly from your business profile to ask product and service questions. As of November 2026, this is limited to eligible US ecommerce and online-service businesses; local, brick-and-mortar listings are restricted to existing US entries for now. OpenAI also says businesses will be able to sponsor their Business Agents and use them as destinations for ChatGPT Ads — so this isn't just an information page, it's a future ad surface too.

With ChatGPT serving 1.2 billion people weekly, setting up a Business Agent correctly means your brand becomes something users chat with directly, not just something they search for. ## What if someone else already claimed your listing?

If your profile's ownership was already assigned to someone else, open the profile's "more options" menu, select "Dispute ownership," and complete Persona verification to prove your authority. This exists specifically to stop someone without authorization from controlling a profile under your brand name; the dispute process runs through the same Persona identity check as a normal claim.

## How does Business Hub differ from a Google Business Profile?

A Google Business Profile gets you visibility in Maps and search results; when someone looks you up, it shows your hours, reviews, and location, but it doesn't converse with them. A Business Agent inside Business Hub answers a user's question directly inside the profile — "does this fit my model," "how long does a return take" — without the user ever visiting your website. The two don't replace each other, they complement each other: showing up in a search result is still Google's job, and answering a follow-up question once you're found is now something ChatGPT can do too. The practical takeaway: you don't drop your Google Business Profile for Business Hub, you run both in parallel, much like you'd run a website and a social account side by side.

## Five things to prepare before you apply

Gathering these in advance keeps the Business Hub application itself under five minutes:

- **Proof of your right to represent the business.** Government ID for Persona, DNS panel access for domain verification, or store admin login for Shopify.
- **Consistent, current business details.** Category, description, and contact info — if these don't match across your website, Google Business Profile, and Business Hub, ChatGPT's mention accuracy suffers.
- **A clear plan for who manages it.** On larger teams, if it's unclear who updates the profile or reviews analytics, verification can stall halfway through.
- **A list of questions for your Business Agent.** If you're eligible, writing out the 10-15 most common customer questions ahead of time speeds up setup.
- **Contact with your rep if you're already an Enterprise customer or advertiser.** In that case, you go through your OpenAI representative directly rather than through Business Hub.

## What should you do now, and what should you wait on?

Do now: apply through Business Hub, claim your listing, finish Persona verification, and fill in your business details — category, description, contact info — completely. Wait on: Business Agents haven't expanded beyond the US or every industry yet, and the pricing model and ad integration details aren't fully settled. My honest take: this is the opening act of "ads inside ChatGPT." Claiming your profile early is free and low-risk, but committing real budget to a Business Agent before the feature reaches your market is premature.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it free to set up a business profile on ChatGPT?

Yes, as of November 2026, claiming a profile and setting up a Business Agent in Business Hub is free. OpenAI says businesses will be able to sponsor their Business Agents and use them as destinations for ChatGPT Ads, which signals a paid tier could be added later.

### Which businesses can set up a Business Agent on ChatGPT?

Right now, only eligible US ecommerce and online-service businesses can set up a Business Agent, and local brick-and-mortar listings are limited to existing US entries. OpenAI may expand this scope, but the region and industry restriction is firm as of this writing.

### What verification methods does ChatGPT Business Hub accept?

Business Hub accepts four verification methods: Persona identity verification, phone verification, domain verification via a TXT record added to your DNS, or linking a Shopify store. You also need to complete Persona identity verification separately before you can publish profile changes, view analytics, or create a Business Agent.

### When does the blue checkmark appear on a ChatGPT business profile?

The blue checkmark appears once you complete Persona identity verification, not when OpenAI approves your ownership claim. These are two distinct steps, so even an approved claim won't show the checkmark or unlock analytics until Persona verification finishes.
